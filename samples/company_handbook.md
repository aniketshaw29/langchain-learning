# Acme Corp Employee Handbook
> The single source of truth for RAG lesson 03. Numbers are made up — that's the point. If the chatbot answers a question *not* in this file, something is wrong. 

## 1. About the company

Acme Corp was founded in **1998** in **Portland, Oregon** by **Dorothy Hughes**. 
It employs roughly **1,200 people** across 3 offices. Acme's mission is *"robots
that are boringly reliable"* — every product is designed to fail *rarely* and to
fail *loudly* when it does.

## 2. Products

- **Widgetron-9**: the flagship industrial actuator. First shipped 2012.
- **SnoozeBot**: a consumer sleep-assist robot. Launched 2018, 40% of revenue.
- **CanoeOS**: the embedded operating system that runs every product. Version 7.2
  introduced our own scheduler, codenamed "Paddle".

The most profitable product is **SnoozeBot** (profit margin 62%). The most
reliable is **Widgetron-9** (1,300,000 mean cycles between failures).

## 4. Benefits

Employees get **25 days** of paid vacation, 10 company holidays, a $300
monthly home-office stipend, and a **free SnoozeBot** on their 1-year
anniversary. The retirement plan is a 6% matching 401(k).

## 5. Team norms

- Every deploy is behind a feature flag for 24 hours.
- "Boring Friday": no new code Friday after 2pm, only bug fixes and docs.
- On-call rotations start Monday, last one week, Unicode-encoded downtime
  reports are required.
- Everyone, regardless of title, spends one day per quarter on the support
  desk. Founder Dorothy still does hers.
